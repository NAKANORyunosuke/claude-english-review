# 数学英語フレーズ集(機能別)

Jerzy Trzeciak, *Writing Mathematical Papers in English* (revised edition, EMS) に基づく抜粋。私的利用の作業用リファレンス。
⟨ ⟩ は交換可能な言い換え、..... は補う箇所。

## 定義・記法の導入

- "A set S is called ⟨said to be⟩ dense if ....."
- "We call m the product measure."(we call の語順は「対象+名前」)
- "We define T to be AB + CD."
- "The function f is given ⟨defined⟩ by f = ....."
- "The length of T, denoted by l(T), is defined to be ....."
- "By the length of T we mean ....."
- "This map is defined by the requirement that f be constant on ....."(要求内容は仮定法現在)
- "Since ....., the norm of f is well defined."
- "Our definition agrees with the one given in [7] if ....."
- "We will denote by Z the set ....." / "Let Z denote ....."(× "Denote f = ...")
- "We write H for the value of ....."
- "Here and subsequently, K denotes ⟨stands for⟩ ....."
- "For simplicity of notation, we write f instead of ....."
- "By abuse of notation, we continue to write f for ....."
- "We will write it simply x when no confusion can arise."
- "We follow the notation of [8]."
- "the Radon–Nikodym property (RNP for short)"(for short は可、in short は不可。briefly は可、shortly は不可)

## 仮定・一般性

- "Unless otherwise stated ⟨Until further notice⟩, we assume that ....."
- "The following assumption will be needed throughout the paper."
- "In the remainder of this section we assume ⟨require⟩ g to be ....."
- "From now on we tacitly assume that ....."
- "Without loss ⟨restriction⟩ of generality we can assume ....."
- "There is no loss of generality in assuming ....." / "This involves no loss of generality."
- "We can assume, by decreasing k if necessary, that ....."
- "We adopt the convention that 0/0 = 0."
- "Now, (4) holds provided ⟨whenever⟩ p ≠ 1." / "..... unless p = 1."
- "This condition cannot be weakened ⟨relaxed/omitted/dropped⟩."
- "The assumption ..... is superfluous ⟨redundant⟩."
- "We will now show how to dispense with the assumption on ....."
- "For definiteness ⟨To be specific⟩, consider ....."
- "There exists a minimal element, say n, of F."
- "Thus F meets S transversally, say at F(0)."

## 定理の主張と参照

- "The theorem states ⟨asserts/shows⟩ that ....."
- "This theorem is an analogue ⟨a partial converse/a sharpened version/a refinement⟩ of ....."
- "Let M be ..... Assume that ..... Then ....., provided m ≠ 1."
- "Under the assumptions of Theorem 2 with "convergent" replaced by "weakly convergent", ....."(仮定の再掲はこの形で圧縮)
- "Under the hypotheses of Theorem 5, if moreover ....., then ....."
- "The following conditions are equivalent: ....."
- "Equality holds in (8) if and only if ....."
- "The theorem is still true if we drop the assumption ....."
- "This specializes to the result of [7] if f = g."
- "We are thus led to the following strengthening of Theorem 6."
- "the following inequality holds" や "We have the following lemma" 型の無情報文は削除できる。

## 証明の開始・段階・終了

- "We first prove a reduced form of the theorem."
- "To see ⟨prove⟩ this, let f = ....."
- "To this end, consider ....."(× to this aim)
- "We claim that ..... Indeed, ....."
- "Our proof starts with the observation that ....."
- "The proof falls naturally into three parts."
- "Since f is compact, we have Lf = 0."(× "Since ....., then .....")
- "By definition ⟨By assumption/By Taylor's formula/By continuity⟩, ....."
- "Theorem 4 now yields ⟨gives/implies⟩ f = ....."
- "From (5) we conclude ⟨deduce/see⟩ that ....." / "From (5) we obtain M = N."(obtain/get/have の後に that 節は付けない)
- "Substituting (4) into (6) ⟨Combining (3) with (6)/Letting n → ∞⟩, we obtain f = g."(分詞の主語は we)
- "Hence ⟨Thus/Consequently,/Therefore⟩ M = N."
- "It suffices to show that ....." / "It remains to prove that ....."
- "The proof is completed by showing that ....."
- "We are now in a position to show ....."
- "We now turn to the case f ≠ 1."
- "Analysis similar to that in the proof of Theorem 5 shows that ....."(× similar as in)
- "The same reasoning applies to the case ....." / "The rest of the proof runs as before."
- "....., which proves the theorem ⟨completes the proof/establishes the formula⟩."(× what proves)
- "....., and the lemma follows."
- "This is the desired conclusion." / "Hence f = g, as claimed ⟨required⟩."
- "Note that we have actually proved that ....."
- "The proof for G is similar." / "The map G may be handled in much the same way."
- "The details are left to the reader." / "We leave it to the reader to verify that ....."(it は必須)

## 帰納法・背理法・場合分け

- "We now proceed by induction." / "The proof is by induction on n."
- "Assume the formula holds for degree k; we will prove it for k + 1."
- "Suppose, contrary to our claim, that ....."
- "To obtain a contradiction, suppose that ....."
- "Suppose the lemma were false. Then we could find ....."
- "If x were not in B, we would have ....."
- "....., which contradicts the maximality of ....." / "....., a contradiction."
- "This contradicts our assumption ⟨the fact that .....⟩."
- "....., which is impossible."(× what is impossible)
- "We need only consider three cases: ....."

- "In either case, it is clear that ....."(= どちらの場合でも)
- "Then either ..... or ..... In the latter ⟨former⟩ case, ....."

- "n is greater than or equal to k."(× greater or equal to)
- "n is no greater than k." / "n is strictly less than k."

- "It is dominated ⟨bounded/estimated/majorized⟩ by ....."
- "The degree of P exceeds that of Q by at least 2."
- "The upper and lower limits of f differ by at most 1."
- "A has twice as many elements as B has." / "half as big as"
- "It is bounded by a multiple of t ⟨a constant times t⟩."
- "..... may be made arbitrarily small by ....."

- "There are only countably many elements q of Q with ....."

## 存在・一意性

- "There exists an x with ....." / "There exist functions R such that ....."

## よくある語法誤り

- a/an は発音で選ぶ: "an M such that", "a unified approach"(× an unified)。
- "Such a map exists."(× Such map exists。ただし "for every such map")
- "in the case of smooth norms"(× in case of)/ "We are in a position to prove"(× in the position)
- 活動を表す名詞は無冠詞: "Application of Definition 5.9 gives (45)." / "Using integration by parts we obtain ....."
- "continuous at x"(× in the point x)
- "disjoint from B" / "equivalent to B" / "independent of B" / "similar to B"(× with / with / on / as)
- "F is equal to G" / "F equals G"(× is equal G)
- "This allows us to prove ....."(× allows to prove)/ "..... enables one ⟨us⟩ to prove"
- "This makes it clear that ....."(× makes clear that)
- "This map satisfies (2)."(× verifies)
- "at the end of Sec. 2"(× in the end of)/ "in Fig. 3"(× on Fig. 3)
- "First, note that"(× At first)/ "Finally,"(× At last)
- "....., which proves our assertion ⟨conclusion⟩."(× thesis = 学位論文)
- "....., as is shown in Sec. 2"(× as it is shown)
- "Let f denote ....." / "Let f satisfy (2)."(× Let f denotes/satisfies)
- "a function bounded by 1"(× a bounded by 1 function)/ "the solution obtained"(× the obtained solution)
- "both its sides"(× its both sides)/ "the first three rows"(× the three first rows)/ "the following two sets"(× the two following sets)
- "We denote this map by f."(× This map we denote by f)
- "Only for x = 1 does the limit exist."(only・否定句が文頭なら倒置)
- "There are a finite number of ....." / "Most of them are ....."(単複一致)
- "We see ⟨conclude/deduce/infer⟩ that B is empty."(× We have ⟨obtain⟩ that)
- "Setting n = p, we can solve the equation by ....."(分詞の主語=主文の主語。× Setting n = p, the equation can be solved)
- "a few examples"(= いくつか)と "few exceptions"(= ほとんどない)を区別する。
- "avoid using"(× avoid to use)/ "succeeded in proving"(× succeeded to prove)/ "finished proving" / "It is worth noting"(× worth to note)
- "We need to consider ....." だが否定・only では "We need not consider" / "we need only consider"。
- 綴り: preceding, occurring, developed, losing, its norm(× preceeding, occuring, developped, loosing, it's norm)

## 相違点(writing-guide.md との)

1. ロードマップ: Trzeciak は Introduction の節案内フレーズ("Section 3 reviews ....." 等)を多数掲載するが、guide はロードマップ段落を書かない方針。
2. 定理の前触れ: Trzeciak は "We now have the following lemma" 型の文を情報がなく削除可とするが、guide は1文の herald を規定。
3. 文頭 Clearly/Obviously/Of course/Indeed: Trzeciak は常用句として掲載、guide はゼロ違反で禁止。
4. 評価・読者管理表現: "It is worth pointing out that", "an interesting formula", "seems to be of independent interest" 等を Trzeciak は掲載、guide は評価形容詞・読者管理修辞を禁止。
5. ヘッジ: "One may conjecture that", "It is to be expected that" を Trzeciak は掲載、guide はヘッジ禁止。
6. 綴り: Trzeciak は英式・米式のどちらでも一貫していれば可(本文は英式)、guide とプロジェクト規約は米式固定。
